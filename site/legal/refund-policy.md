# Refund Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## Fourteen days, any reason, no questions asked

If CatalogOps is not what you wanted, tell us within **14 days** of your purchase
and you get **100% of your money back**. You do not have to explain why, and you
will not be asked to.

That is the whole promise. The rest of this page is only detail.

## Why it is worded that way

CatalogOps writes to your prices. Asking someone to trust unfamiliar software
with that, from a developer they have never heard of, is asking a lot — and a
guarantee that arrives with conditions attached is not much of a guarantee.

So there are no conditions. Not "if you found a bug we could not fix". Not "if
you have not downloaded it yet". You changed your mind; that is reason enough.

## How to ask

Email **contact@catalog-ops.app** from the address you bought with, or use the
contact option in your Freemius account. One line is enough.

What then happens, in order:

1. Your subscription is cancelled, so nothing renews.
2. Your licence is cancelled.
3. The full amount is refunded.

We do not delay it to ask what went wrong. If we ask anything, it is **after** the
money has gone back, it is one question, and you are free to ignore it.

The refund is issued by **Freemius**, who is the merchant of record for the sale,
and lands back on the card or account you paid with. How long it takes to appear
depends on your bank — usually a few working days.

## After the fourteen days

The guarantee period is over, but you are not stuck with anything:

- **Cancel the subscription** at any time from your Freemius account and it will
  not renew.
- **The plugin keeps working** after a licence lapses. You stop getting updates
  and support; you do not lose your catalogue tools, your operation history, or
  the ability to undo a run you made months ago.

## Renewals

A subscription renews once a year unless you cancel it. Freemius emails you
before it does.

If a renewal takes you by surprise, write to us. We are not interested in keeping
money from someone who did not mean to spend it, and this is a case where we will
simply look at it rather than point at a date.

Questions: **contact@catalog-ops.app**
