# Cookie Policy

**Last updated: [DATE]**

## This website sets no cookies

Not "only essential ones". None.

That is why there is no consent banner on this site. A banner exists to ask
permission to put something on your device; there is nothing here to ask about.

## What that took

A site sets no cookies only on purpose, so here is what was deliberately left
out:

- **Fonts are served from our own server**, not from Google Fonts. Loading them
  from Google would send your IP address there before you had any say in it.
- **Audience measurement is [Plausible](https://plausible.io)**, hosted in the
  EU, which counts visits without a cookie and without any identifier that
  follows you. It cannot tell one reader from another; it only knows a page was
  read.
- **No embedded videos, maps, social widgets or avatars.** These are the usual
  quiet culprits — one embedded player is enough to put a third party's cookies
  on your device on a page that claims to have none.
- **No advertising or tracking pixels**, of any kind.
- **Comments are disabled**, because a WordPress comment form stores your name
  and email on your device for the next time.

## Where cookies do appear, and why it is not this site

**When you buy.** Checkout runs on `checkout.freemius.com`, which is Freemius's
service, not ours. That page needs cookies to hold your basket and complete the
payment, and it is covered by
[Freemius's own policy](https://freemius.com/privacy/). It is a different site;
we mention it so the transition does not surprise you.

**Inside your own WordPress admin.** The CatalogOps plugin runs in your
dashboard, where WordPress sets its own login and session cookies. Those belong
to your installation and have nothing to do with this website. The plugin adds
none of its own.

## Server logs are not cookies

Our web server writes an ordinary access log — your IP address, the time, and the
page requested. That is a record kept on our side, not something stored on your
device, so it needs no consent. It is described in the
[Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/), which also says how long it is kept.

## If this ever changes

If we add anything that stores data on your device, this page changes first and a
consent request appears with it — before the thing that needs consent, not after.
