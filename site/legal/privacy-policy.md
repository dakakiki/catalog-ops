## What the website collects

**Server logs.** Every request to `catalog-ops.app` is written to an ordinary
web-server log: IP address, date and time, the page requested, the referring
page, and the browser's user-agent string. These exist to keep the site running
and to investigate abuse.

- Legal basis: legitimate interest in operating and securing the site.
- Retention: **[N] days**, then deleted.

**Audience measurement (Plausible).** Visits are counted with
[Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io), hosted in the European Union.
Plausible sets no cookies, stores no persistent identifier, and does not follow
you between websites. It records the page, the referrer, the country, and the
device type as **aggregate counts** — there is no profile, and no way to single
you out from what it keeps.

- Legal basis: legitimate interest in knowing which pages are read.
- Because nothing is stored on your device and no identifier is kept, this does
  not require your consent — which is precisely why it was chosen over the
  alternatives.

**Fonts and other assets** are served from our own server. No request goes to
Google, to a CDN, or to any other third party while you read this site.

## What happens when you buy

Purchases go through **Freemius Inc.**, which is the **seller and merchant of
record**. Your purchase contract is with Freemius. They take the payment, issue
the invoice, handle tax, and process your billing details.

**We never receive or store your card details.** What reaches us is the
information needed to support a licence: your email address, the plan you bought,
and the sites your licence is activated on.

Freemius acts as an independent controller for the payment itself. Their handling
of your data is governed by [Freemius's privacy policy](https://freemius.com/privacy/).

## What the plugin sends, and only if you allow it

When CatalogOps is activated, Freemius shows a screen asking permission to
connect your site. **It has a "Skip" button, and skipping costs you nothing in
the plugin's day-to-day work.**

If — and only if — you choose *Allow*, the following is sent to Freemius:

- your site's URL and the email address of the administrator who activated it,
- the WordPress and PHP versions,
- a list of active plugins and themes,
- and, later, licence activations and deactivations.

This is used for licensing, update delivery, and support. **If you skip, none of
it is sent** — the plugin runs, filters, previews and applies exactly the same.
The one thing you give up is automatic update notices, because the plugin has no
way to ask whether a new version exists without identifying itself.

You can withdraw this at any time from the plugin's own settings.

- Legal basis: your consent.

**CatalogOps never sends your product data anywhere.** Prices, stock, SKUs,
customer records and orders stay in your database. The plugin reads and writes
them on your own server and transmits none of it.

## International transfers

**Nothing this site collects is stored outside the European Economic Area.** The
server, its logs and the mailbox that receives your messages are all with Hetzner
in Germany. Audience measurement is with Plausible in the European Union.

We are established in Serbia, which is outside the EEA and is **not** covered by
an adequacy decision of the European Commission. We reach the data described
above by logging in to those EEA services from Serbia; it is not copied here in
the ordinary course. That access is made on the basis of **[TRANSFER BASIS]**.

The GDPR and the UK GDPR apply to us directly, because CatalogOps is offered to
customers in those territories.

**Freemius Inc.** is the one service outside the EEA: it is in the United States,
and its data-processing agreement incorporates the European Commission's Standard
Contractual Clauses of 2021 for transfers.

## Your rights

If the GDPR or UK GDPR applies to you, you may ask us to give you a copy of what
we hold, correct it, delete it, restrict what we do with it, or object to it —
and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw

Write to **contact@catalog-ops.app**. We will answer within one month.

You may also complain to a supervisory authority: in the EU, the one in your
country of residence; in Serbia, the Commissioner for Information of Public
Importance and Personal Data Protection.
